Design Rules & Brand Voice

Aligned with DESIGN.md and the blue twin-cat identity
Dimension Standard Never Do
Brand Colour Use Déjà View Blue (#0A84FF / #007AFF) with ice-blue surfaces and obsidian ink. Never use purple gradients or purple brand accents.
Logo & Metaphor Twin black cats inside the blue magnifying glass symbolize seeing a post twice and finding it instantly. Never stretch the lens or crop the cats out of the ring.
UI Chrome Native iOS 26/27 Liquid Glass via NavigationStack, TabView, LazyVGrid, and sheets. Never draw custom tab bars or custom fonts.
Platform Attribution Plain .caption2 text label (“Instagram”, “TikTok”, “Threads”, “X”, “Facebook”, “Web”). Never use trademarked third-party platform logos.
Copy & Tone Sentence case, warm, concise, human (“Processing…”, “All caught up”). Never expose technical jargon (“Inferencing”, “Vectorizing”).
